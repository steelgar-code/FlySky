    Settings

    For the mode chosen in the bottom bar (Ensemble or Models).

    Flight profile

    A flight from the forecast location: climb, cruise, descent. The altitude (above the takeoff point) is also used by the wind aloft in Models mode, the map's wind arrows and the flight time calculator.

    Screens the models' atmosphere along that flight for sub-zero cloud, freezing fog, freezing rain and drizzle (DWD ICON and UK Met Office, ≈ 4 Open-Meteo calls every 3 hours). Not a validated icing forecast: no icing signs never means safe.

    Units

    Flight thresholds

    Suitable (green) below the first value, not suitable (red) from the second; in between is uncertain (yellow). The wind limits are for the 10 m wind and gusts; wind aloft has limits of its own. Visibility is the other way round: suitable above the first value, not suitable below the second.

    Flight Window Planner

    Flyable periods (suitable + uncertain) shorter than this are left out of the planner.

    Nearby hazards

    A chip on the flight card when satellite data shows lightning or rain within 50 km of the forecast location.
