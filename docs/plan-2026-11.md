## 4. Open decisions

1. **Risk tolerance of the headline verdict.** Proposed: a setting with Cautious (not suitable from a 10 % share of members), Balanced (30 %, default) and Median-like (50 %, close to today); the confidence then reads "Suitable — 82 % of members agree". Or keep today's median verdict and change only the confidence wording.
2. **Rain.** Proposed per member: ≥ 0.1 mm/h uncertain, ≥ 1 mm/h not suitable, instead of today's chance limits (0 % / 40 %).
3. **Gust limits.** The review suggests 10 / 16 m/s (mean wind stays 8 / 14).
4. **Wind aloft.** Models mode: follow the flight altitude (≈ 2.5 more calls per update)? Ensemble mode: 925 hPa only (≈ 600 m, labelled), or each member's stronger level with 1000 hPa left out where it is underground?
5. **Aircraft types.** Fixed-wing only, or multirotors too (the calculator's airspeed range, aircraft profiles)?
6. **Lockfile and CI.** Commit `tests/package-lock.json` and add a GitHub Actions workflow?
